📖 Cara Belajar / How to Study

1. Baca bahasa Indonesia dulu, lalu English. Read Indonesian first, then English.

2. Lihat gambar sebelum membaca langkah. Look at the picture before reading the steps.

3. Latihan bersama perawat/instruktur, jangan langsung ke pasien. Practice with a nurse/instructor, not directly on a patient first.

4. Hafalkan kotak merah ⚠️ (tanda bahaya). Memorize the red ⚠️ boxes (danger signs).

Bahan belajar untuk calon caregiver. Bukan pengganti pelatihan langsung. Selalu ikuti instruksi dokter/perawat.
Study material for caregiver trainees. Not a replacement for hands-on training. Always follow your doctor/nurse's instructions.
